Evidence and method

A design built on technical dossiers

The design of Semillas de lectura rests on seven technical dossiers that bring together the evidence, the decisions and the validation strategy, and on a twenty-chapter book that explains the argument to the reader. It is a complete architecture, not yet validated in the field.

What to look for in the method

Three guiding ideas

  • Two descriptions, not one band. The text is described by its code and by its language; the child never receives a fixed level.
  • A task route, not a scale. Assessment yields a teaching priority and a state of the evidence, not a score.
  • Variety is not error. What preserves the word in the reader's variety is not penalized.
A complete architecture is not a validated result.

The status of every decision, hypothesis and open issue is registered and kept separate in the documentation control. No hypothesis is presented as a result.
