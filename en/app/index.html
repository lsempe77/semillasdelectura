Tracking app

The classroom map, in an app

Teachers sign in with a PIN and see where every student stands: their level in each reading and writing skill. The app flags children who fall back or stall, and suggests what to work on. Progress is recorded in one tap.

Today's plan

The app suggests which student to look at first, based on alerts and pending records. It suggests the skill too: the teacher never opens a list.

Classroom map

Each student is a row and each skill a column. Colour shows the level: achieved, in progress, starting, or no data.

Student card

The level per skill, the trend, the history and the alerts: fell back, stalled, needs support, or no recent record.

Two ways to record

Mark the level in one tap, or apply a short individual test (four questions) that confirms the level.

Under construction, with honest status.

The app is a minimum version that runs on demonstration data. Its pedagogical content (skills, observation rubric, suggestions and item bank) is in proposed status, pending validation. The companion module, offline use and monitoring dashboards are not built yet.
