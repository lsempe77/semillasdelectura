The collection

Books designed for a purpose

Eighteen titles produced and grouped by world. Every book is described in two ways, by the code demand it makes and by its language profile, never by a single band that labels the child.

C

What it demands: code

Every title states its demand within the reference C sequence, the repertoire the text may use. The sequence orders code from the base band (C1) to the most demanding bands of the first series (C3 and C4). A C1 text relies on a narrow repertoire; a C4 text brings in structures that earlier bands do not cover.

L

How it reads: the language profile

Vocabulary, syntax, discourse, knowledge and image. Two titles can share a code and differ in language: the series alternates narrative and information, and grades each dimension separately. The profile describes the text, not the reader.

The series

Nina the vizcacha and her worlds

The series follows Nina the vizcacha across Andean settings. Worlds group titles by setting and cast, and order production. The first world, «The hillside», is complete (14 of 14 titles).
